# Explorable chapter worlds

Six distinct connected maps, each 4400 x 3000 world units, replace the campaign arena. Each contains six named landmarks, branching paths, four enemy camps, a one-use healing spring, distributed objectives, and a separate boss destination. Layouts: Remembering Wilds, Prism Coast, Lunar Stair, Crooked Fairgrounds, Cinder Expanse, Unwritten Reach.

Movement is constrained to clearings and connecting paths. Long clicks route via the landmark graph. Keyboard movement, dash, companion movement, knockback and placed spells respect the same world boundaries. A following camera transforms mouse aim into world coordinates; the minimap shows routes, discoveries, memory objectives and bosses. Distant enemies sleep beyond 900 units. Camps activate once when approached. Carnival survival advances only within its stage radius.

Scenery is new procedural Canvas art with biome-specific props, terrain texture and landmark labels. No new painted world artwork or finished 3D environments are claimed. Original character and companion assets are preserved. The original grove remains available separately under /grove/.

31 tests passed: original roster tests, six-chapter combat completion, prerequisite and reward rules, all landmarks reached on walkable paths in all maps, casts and dashes outside old bounds for seven characters, camera clamps, and carnival location requirements. Combat harness grants resources/invulnerability to verify logic, not difficulty. Browser verification follows deployment.
