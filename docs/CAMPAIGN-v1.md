# The Unwritten Signal — campaign prototype

Six playable mission chapters, with the Prism Undertow and Sleepless Observatory available in either order after the grove. Both must be completed before the carnival, archive, and Curator finale. Every selected keeper has a separate campaign save. First-clear rewards are capped by chapter completion, survive defeat, and do not stack on replays. Mission progress restarts after defeat or reload; completed chapters persist on this browser.

Objectives: three anchors; ten kills then relay; two anchors with falling clock strikes; 35-second survival then relay; three burning archive anchors; two final locks followed by Curator combat. Later missions add telegraphed area hazards. All bosses currently reuse the guardian model and base attacks; final encounter adds three danger circles. This is not six finished environments. Region color washes and story scenes reuse the grove arena and existing art.

The Curator tried to prevent grief by preserving one perfect day. The keepers recover growth, possibility, dreams, unscripted laughter and painful memories before breaking its defense. The ending choice gives the Curator a new life as a gardener or lets it leave through the open veil. No copied names, plot, maps or dialogue from the inspiration games.

## Inspiration

- Official Last Epoch explanation of chapters versus eras (historical design explanation, 2019): https://forum.lastepoch.com/t/how-to-get-new-chapters/15665 — a familiar place can carry different meanings across world states. Future production work can turn the shared prototype arena into visibly transformed regions.
- Official PoE2 Third Edict notes (0.3.0, historical): https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3826682/filter-account-type/staff — Act Four offers islands in a player-chosen order. Quetopia uses a small two-route fork followed by a story reunion.
- Last Epoch player feedback: https://forum.lastepoch.com/t/quest-rewards-detailed-on-quest-acceptance/72266 — asks for clearer quest rewards. Quetopia states the permanent first-clear reward before mission entry. This is player feedback, not an official design claim.

## Verification

22 simulation tests passed, including all six campaign missions through combat, branch prerequisites, repeat-reward protection, defeat/retry and hazard warning timing. Combat harness provides invulnerability/resources to test completion logic; this does not establish difficulty balance or human completion time. Browser verification is recorded separately after deployment.
