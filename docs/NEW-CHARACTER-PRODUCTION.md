## Production gates
1. Inspect source, establish full-body silhouette and turnaround; unseen anatomy is an adaptation.
2. Sculpt and render front/side/back for visual review. Do not label primitive placeholders final art.
3. Retopology, UVs, material baking and LODs; establish budget from actual game profiling, not arbitrary polygon counts.
4. Articulated rig, idle/locomotion/attack/hit/death; check foot contacts and smooth turns.
5. Preview one pair in the existing full game, preserve source and compare scale against original druid.
6. Only then repeat validated process for remaining pairs.

The earlier requested PoE2/Last Epoch balance research is still pending. Proposed roles above are design hypotheses, not research conclusions. Budget the character and companion as one combined combat kit; compare clear speed, boss damage, survival and mobility across matched encounters.
